Install

Your download is a zip with every plugin format inside. Unzip it, then copy the formats you use into place. When in doubt, install VST3 and the standalone app.

macOS

Copy each bundle to the folder for its format, then restart your DAW:

  • Cartridge.app (standalone) → /Applications
  • Cartridge.component (AU) → /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  • Cartridge.vst3 → /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  • Cartridge.clap → /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP

The build is signed and notarised, so macOS opens it without warnings. If your DAW doesn't see the AU right away, Logic and GarageBand sometimes need a full quit and relaunch to rescan.

Windows
  • Cartridge.vst3 → C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
  • Cartridge.clap → C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP
  • Cartridge.exe (standalone) → anywhere you like

Rescan plugins in your DAW after copying.

Linux
  • Cartridge.vst3 → ~/.vst3
  • Cartridge.clap → ~/.clap
  • Cartridge (standalone) → anywhere on your path

Activate

Your licence key arrives by email right after purchase, from licences@staycoolandstaycool.com. Check spam if it hasn't landed within a few minutes.

  1. Open Cartridge (any format) and click the DEMO badge in the top bar.
  2. Paste your licence key and click Activate.
  3. Done. Activation needs the internet once; after that the licence works fully offline.

One licence covers up to 3 machines. Activate on each machine the same way, with the same key.

Moving machines

Retiring a computer or hit the 3-machine limit? Click the LICENCE badge on the old machine and choose Deactivate. That frees the seat so you can activate elsewhere. If the old machine is dead and you can't deactivate, write to support and we'll free it for you.

Help

Stuck on anything, or the key won't take? Email support@staycoolandstaycool.com with your order number and we'll sort it out. The Operator's Manual covers every control in depth, and the FAQ covers system requirements and DAW compatibility.
