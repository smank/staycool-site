§ 004 / About

Hi, I’m smank.

I love music, art, and games, and the 8-bit era most of all. Cartridge is my love letter to those sounds. When I’m not making software I’m on a guitar or a synth, playing it or taking it apart. I set them up, rewire them, rebuild them, and solder things that were probably fine before I got involved. I build the tools I want to use, by hand, and there’s nobody upstairs asking me to put AI in a synth.
